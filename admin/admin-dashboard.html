Workflow Automations — what happens automatically

TriggerWhat happens automatically
Customer submits a registrationCreates the Customer ID and registration record, sends the customer a confirmation and alerts staff that a review is pending
Customer submits a payment screenshotStatus becomes "Under Verification" and staff get a verification alert
Admin approves the registrationRegistration ID + Invoice number generate, customer ko notification, project assign
Admin approves the paymentA receipt number is generated and the customer gets a receipt notification
Admin approves the advance paymentPayment record update + customer ko notification (transaction ref ke saath)
Courier status changesCustomer is notified at every stage; on "Delivered" the project stage becomes "Materials Sent"
Customer submits the completed projectA pickup request is created, staff are alerted and the quality check stage begins
Parcel reaches the officeProject moves to "Under Quality Check" and staff are alerted
Quality is approvedCustomer ko notification, payment staff ko "final payment pending" alert
Final payment is approvedProject is automatically marked "Completed" and the customer is notified
Deadline is approachingDeadline se pehle, deadline ke din, aur overdue hone par reminders
