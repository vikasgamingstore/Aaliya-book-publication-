Workflow Automations — kya apne aap hota hai

Jab ye hota haiAutomatically ye hota hai
Customer registration submit karta haiCustomer ID + registration record bante hain, customer ko confirmation, staff ko "review pending" alert
Customer payment screenshot submit karta haiStatus "Under Verification" hota hai, staff ko verification alert jaata hai
Admin registration approve karta haiRegistration ID + Invoice number generate, customer ko notification, project assign
Admin payment approve karta haiReceipt number generate hota hai, customer ko receipt notification
Admin advance payment approve karta haiPayment record update + customer ko notification (transaction ref ke saath)
Courier status badalta haiHar stage par customer ko notification; "Delivered" par project stage "Materials Sent" ho jaata hai
Customer project complete submit karta haiPickup request banti hai, staff ko alert, quality check stage shuru
Parcel office pahunchta haiProject "Under Quality Check" ho jaata hai, staff ko alert
Quality approve hota haiCustomer ko notification, payment staff ko "final payment pending" alert
Final payment approve hota haiProject apne aap "Completed" mark hota hai, customer ko confirmation
Deadline paas aati haiDeadline se pehle, deadline ke din, aur overdue hone par reminders
